Results

Per share fair value and investment note, base case CHF 0.89 vs market CHF 4.35

TL;DR

Base case fair value: CHF 0.89 / share. Current market: CHF 4.35 (mid June 2026). Apparent downside: −80 %.

But the number to remember is not −80 %. The 80 % apparent gap is what the market is paying for three catalysts the base case deliberately excludes: (1) a residual claim on the ring fenced SARL assets (aprocitentan / selatogrel / cenerimod), (2) a QUVIVIQ blockbuster trajectory north of CHF 600 M peak, (3) equity optionality on the J&J convertible. The reconciled fair value therefore sits anywhere in CHF 0.9 – 6.6 per share depending on how one weights those catalysts. A “Base + modest SARL residual (CHF 400 M)” reads at CHF ~2.5 / share.

They should be read as “peak sales assumption too conservative to justify the ongoing spend”, not as “the program should be discontinued”, Phase 2 PoC readouts (CCR6 in Q4 2026) will reanchor those figures materially in either direction.

From cash to per share value

Sum of product rNPVs      : CHF   727 M
+ Parent net cash          : CHF  −504 M   (cash 69 M − attributable debt 573 M)
+ SARL residual (base = 0) : CHF     0 M
= Enterprise value (parent): CHF   223 M
÷ Shares outstanding       :        250.7 M
= Fair value / share       : CHF   0.89

Parent net cash is negative because we conservatively attribute CHF 573 M of debt (J&J convertible + convertible bonds + other financial debt including R-Bridge royalty monetization) to the parent equity holder against only ~CHF 69 M of usable cash. The CHF 762 M of SARL notes are excluded, they are non recourse to IDIA shareholders and are serviced by the SARL assets (see Methodology).

What the valuation rides on

Two variables dominate, nearly tied, the SARL residual assumption (0 vs CHF 400 M swings the model from CHF 0.89 to CHF 2.48 / share) and QUVIVIQ peak sales (±30 % swings from CHF 0.09 to CHF 1.69). Third is the QUVIVIQ operating margin (±5 pp). Discount rate is a mid tier lever; lucerastat peak sales and PoS matter but do not move the needle on their own.

Critical read of the tornado: no single variable at its stress case reaches the current market price. Closing the gap requires multiple positive assumptions simultaneously, that is the market’s implicit bet.

To reach the current CHF 4.35 with QUVIVIQ alone requires peak sales CHF ~1 000-1 100 M and a discount rate at the low end of the WACC range (9-10 %). That is not implausible, descheduling in the US, IDSIQ label enhancement, and pediatric approval, if all three land, plausibly get QUVIVIQ into that territory, but each is a separate probabilistic event.

Verdict

The market is not wrong; the base case is not wrong. They price different things.

The base case values only the parent owned assets, uses industry standard Phase 2 to approval and Phase 3 to approval probabilities, and applies a conservative peak sales estimate to a genuinely differentiated but uncertain commercial asset. It answers the question: “If none of the three catalysts materialise, what is Idorsia worth?” The answer is CHF ~0.9 / share.

The market answers the different question: “What is Idorsia worth on a probability weighted basis, including some credit for the SARL reversion and for QUVIVIQ becoming a genuine blockbuster?” The reconciled fair value in that framework sits somewhere in CHF 2.5 – 5 / share. The market is at CHF 4.35, the upper end of that range.

My reconciled personal read: at CHF 4.35, IDIA is pricing in a material SARL residual plus a QUVIVIQ trajectory noticeably above the CHF 600 M I use as base. That is a fairly optimistic combination which is not irrational, but leaves limited margin of safety at the current level. It is not an obvious buy at these levels; it is not obviously a sell either. It is a name whose fair value critically depends on things that will be known in the next 18-24 months, the CCR6 PoC readout (Q4 2026), the US QUVIVIQ commercial trajectory, and progress on repaying the SARL notes.

Limitations

  • All figures are constrained by the quality of the peak sales, PoS and discount rate inputs.
  • Peak sales for the three Phase 2 candidates are placeholders and drive their negative rNPVs. A positive CCR6 PoC readout at Q4 2026 would materially reshape those figures.
  • The J&J convertible is treated as pure debt at its CHF ~11.55 strike (deep OTM at current prices). Rerating would turn it into an equity dilution event.
  • Working capital and capex are netted to zero; tax proxied by EBIT margin; product outcomes are assumed independent.
  • The SARL contingent liability to J&J (30 % of aprocitentan proceeds + 10 % each of selatogrel and cenerimod) is disclosed but not fully modelled, a known refinement.
  • Modelling conventions (mid year discount, 8 year QUVIVIQ ramp) are documented in Methodology §Modelling conventions.

Don’t read the CHF 0.89 as a price target. Read the sourcing and the scenarios. Every input is traceable to a public document, every scenario has an explicit narrative, and every CHF of the gap between the model and the market can be tied to a specific bet.
