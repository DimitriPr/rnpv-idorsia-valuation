Results

Per-share fair value and investment note

TL;DR

Base case fair value: CHF 2.46 / share (floored at zero). Equity value CHF +750 M pre-floor; current market CHF 4.16 (IDIA.SW close 2026-10-13). Valuation date: 2026-06-30.

The base case is a conservative lower bound, not a price target. The gap to the market decomposes into two catalysts the base case excludes: (1) a residual claim on the ring-fenced SARL assets (aprocitentan / selatogrel / cenerimod), and (2) a QUVIVIQ blockbuster trajectory above the base-case peak. On top of that sits one refinancing event the base case already treats as pure debt: the J&J convertible loan (CHF 250 M, matures 15 Jun 2027, convertible into 21.7 M shares at CHF 11.48). The convertible is never an upside: either dilution if IDIA rerates above the strike, or a cash repayment at maturity.

Reconciled fair-value range across scenarios: CHF 0.31 - 9.36 / share (post-floor, post-if-converted). This is the single range used across all pages of this note.

Parent net cash is negative because we attribute CHF 361 M of debt to the parent equity holder (J&J convertible 0 + Pharmakon term loan 150 + convertible bonds 49 + other financial 162 incl. R-Bridge royalty monetization) against only CHF 105 M of group cash (an upper bound at 30 Jun 2026 - see TODO_DATA.md). The CHF 775 M of SARL notes are excluded, they are non-recourse to IDIA shareholders and are serviced by the SARL assets.

The SARL New Money Facility (CHF 150 M nominal, CHF 78 M drawn at YE 2025, CHF 105 M drawn at repayment) was repaid in full on 26 Jun 2026 from the first Pharmakon tranche, so it does not appear in the current debt line.

What the valuation rides on

The dominant levers in the base case are QUVIVIQ peak sales (+/-30 %) (swing CHF 2.56; low CHF 1.18, high CHF 3.75) and QUVIVIQ peak contribution margin (+/-10 pp) (swing CHF 1.65; low CHF 1.63, high CHF 3.28).

Mid-tier levers: SARL residual (0 vs CHF 400 M) (swing CHF 1.31; low CHF 2.46, high CHF 3.77); Discount rate (10 % vs 14 %) (swing CHF 1.18; low CHF 1.92, high CHF 3.10).

Minor levers (not sufficient to move the needle on their own): Lucerastat peak sales (+/-30 %) (swing CHF 0.71; low CHF 2.10, high CHF 2.81); Lucerastat PoS (+/-20 %) (swing CHF 0.47; low CHF 2.22, high CHF 2.69); Total central costs (+/-25 %) (swing CHF 0.44; low CHF 2.21, high CHF 2.65); Lucerastat peak contribution margin (+/-10 pp) (swing CHF 0.34; low CHF 2.29, high CHF 2.63).

Critical read: no single variable at its stress case reaches the current market price of CHF 4.16. Closing the gap requires multiple positive assumptions simultaneously, that is the market's implicit bet.

DEA Schedule V update (not full descheduling)

On 11 Aug 2026, the DEA proposed to reclassify DORAs (QUVIVIQ, Belsomra, Dayvigo) from Schedule IV to Schedule V, not a full descheduling. Schedule V is the least-restrictive controlled substances tier (looser prescribing / refill constraints) but still a scheduled drug, so prescriber dispensing frictions are reduced without being eliminated. The upside scenarios in this note treat the reclassification as a modest commercial accelerator, not as a transformational catalyst. Source: Marktpuls, Aug 2026.

Limitations

  • rNPV is an order-of-magnitude tool, not a precise valuation.
  • Phase 2 peak sales (CCR6, CXCR7, CXCR3) are placeholders and drive their negative rNPVs. A positive CCR6 PoC readout at Q4 2026 would materially reshape those figures.
  • The J&J convertible is a cost, not an upside: either dilution (if V/share > strike at maturity) or a CHF 335 M cash repayment on 15 Jun 2027. The if-converted test applies in scenarios where V/share > strike.
  • Central costs are modelled as a decline-then-floor-then-proportional curve (71 M G&A sourced + 25 M upstream placeholder at year 1, decay 10 %/year to floor 50 M to 2040, then scaled by weighted revenue to 2050 with hard floor 10 M). Not a line-by-line projection.
  • Upstream research is deducted but no value is attributed to the unborn pipeline candidates that upstream research will generate. This biases the model downward.
  • QUVIVIQ post-launch R&D (pediatric programme, label extensions) is not modelled in either the per-product dev-cost curve (prelaunch only) or central costs.
  • Working capital and capex are netted to zero; tax proxied by the operating margin; product outcomes are assumed independent.
  • The SARL contingent liability to J&J (30 % aprocitentan + 10 % each selatogrel / cenerimod proceeds) is disclosed but not fully modelled, a known refinement.
  • All historical data and the current share price are pinned to specific dates (data/market.json for the quote; A.VALUATION_DATE = 2026-06-30 for the balance sheet). Re-run scripts/update_price.py before each deploy.

Don't read any point estimate as a price target. Read the sourcing and the scenarios: every input has a status (sourced / assumption / placeholder), every scenario has an explicit narrative, and every CHF of the gap between the model and the market can be tied to a specific bet.
